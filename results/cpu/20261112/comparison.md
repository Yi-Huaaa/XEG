## Reading the measurements

CPU thread counts refer to **tree/DAG construction threads**, separately from CP-SAT workers. CPU overall runtime is the recorded `cpu_pipeline_ms`: tree construction, DAG construction, validation, solver formulation/search, and final solution selection. It excludes input loading/JSON parsing and output-file writing; the CSV also supplies process elapsed time for the quality run. The paper runtime is transcribed unchanged; its precise input-parsing timing boundary was not specified in the inspected runtime discussion.

The quality sweep ran up to eight independent inputs concurrently in its allocation. Its recorded overall wall times are not an isolated scaling benchmark. The two CPU construction tables below report separate tree and DAG measurements for scaling. The paper reports an Intel Core i5-13500 plus RTX A4000 setup and aggregation across runs; these CPU runs used the current Slurm environment. Hardware, solver settings, and aggregation differ, so the runtime ratios in the quality table are descriptive observations. Published paper numbers are used directly, without attempting to reconstruct the author's tuning or selection of runs.

## CPU status

`CPU status` describes the source of the final solution and whether its optimality was proved. The key applies to the quality table and the supplementary fixed-work results retained in the CSV.

| Status | Final solution | Optimality guarantee |
|---|---|---|
| **O — Optimal** | A legal solution returned by CP-SAT. | Proven optimal within the candidate space produced by tree/DAG construction. |
| **F — Feasible** | A legal solution returned by CP-SAT. | Optimality has not been proved. |
| **T — Tree fallback** | The validated seed tree is retained because CP-SAT found no feasible solution or returned one with a higher cost than the seed. | Optimality has not been proved. |

**O does not imply a global optimum over the original, complete e-graph.** Construction restricts the candidate space searched by CP-SAT.

**All final CPU solutions in the quality table and CSV, including O, F, and T, passed independent legality verification.** F and T do not indicate an illegal solution; they indicate the absence of an optimality proof, with T additionally identifying the fallback source.
